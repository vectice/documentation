# 👤 Your Vectice Account

Your journey in Vectice begins with your account. If you do not have an account, [contact us](https://www.vectice.com/contact), and we will help set it up for you.&#x20;

When an administrator invites you to Vectice, you will typically receive an email from Vectice with a link to accept the invitation. You will be asked to provide a password as you sign-up.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
Contact your admin, if you did not receive an email invitation. Your organization may not have enabled emails from Vectice.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

Once you provide the password and log in to Vectice, you will be in the home page of the web app, from where you will be able to learn more about Vectice.&#x20;

**Setup Your Profile**

From the top-right corner of the web UI, visit your user profile. Here you will be able to:&#x20;

* Update your profile, including a profile picture, full name and a short bio about yourself.&#x20;
* Manage your API tokens if you intend to use that interface.
* Change your password, if you wish to.&#x20;

That's it, you are all set with your Vectice account! Next let's understand the key Vectice concepts.&#x20;
